Privacy Policy & Patient Confidentiality

This Privacy Policy outlines how Shree Krishna Dental Hospital (led by Dr. Mann V. Thummar) collects, uses, and safeguards your medical & personal information when you visit our clinic or use our website.

1. Introduction

Your trust is essential to us. When you schedule a treatment or consult Dr. Mann V. Thummar at Shree Krishna Dental Hospital, your personal details and medical records are handled with utmost confidentiality and high ethical standards.

2. Information We Collect

To provide high-quality dental care and seamless appointment scheduling, we collect:

2.1 Personal & Contact Information

  • Full Name, Age, Gender
  • Phone Number and WhatsApp contact details
  • Email address
  • Residential address

2.2 Medical & Dental History

  • Dental diagnostic details, X-rays, and treatment plans
  • Past medical history, allergies, and ongoing medications
  • Appointment notes and consultation records

3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is strictly used for clinical care and communication:

  • Diagnosing dental conditions and providing personalized dental treatments
  • Sending appointment confirmations, reminders, and updates via SMS/WhatsApp
  • Maintaining secure clinical health records for future reference
  • Responding to inquiries or consultation requests

4. Information Security & Confidentiality

We strictly protect patient confidentiality. We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal or medical data to third parties.

Medical data is shared only with authorized medical professionals or diagnostic laboratories when required directly for your treatment plan, or if required by applicable medical regulations.

5. Data Protection & Storage

All digital records and communication are secured against unauthorized access. Physical records at our Surat clinic are stored safely under professional clinical supervision.

6. Patient Rights

As a patient of Shree Krishna Dental Hospital, you have the right to:

  • Access and request copies of your dental records or X-rays
  • Update or correct your personal contact details
  • Opt out of non-essential reminders or notifications

7. Policy Updates

We may periodically update this policy to reflect improvements in patient privacy standards. Updated terms will be published directly on this website.
