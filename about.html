Dr. Mann V. Thummar (B.D.S., M.D.S.)

Consultant Prosthodontist & Implantologist

For over 20 years, Shree Krishna Dental Hospital has been providing trusted, gentle, and high-quality superspeciality dental care in Surat. Located at Sarthana Jakatnaka, our hospital is equipped with advanced digital X-ray technology, strict sterilization standards, and modern treatment rooms.

Under the expert guidance of Dr. Mann V. Thummar, we specialize in Dental Implants, Single-Sitting Root Canals, Crowns & Bridges, Full Mouth Dentures, and General Oral Care.

Our Journey

Ahmedabad Beginnings

Over the years, our practice grew along with the vibrant communities of Varachha, Katargam, Puna, and Parvat Patiya, with Dental Implantology becoming a special focus of our clinical practice.
