🌍 FOR OVERSEAS PATIENTS

NRI-Friendly Dental Treatment

Quality Dental Care, Planned Around Your Time

We understand that patients living overseas often have limited time in India. Our treatment approach is therefore designed to make the dental journey as planned, organized and time-efficient as possible.

Time-Efficient Treatment Planning

Treatment is planned systematically to minimize unnecessary visits and make the best use of the patient's available time in India.

Advance Treatment Planning

Where possible, diagnosis, treatment requirements and appointment schedules are planned beforehand so patients can organize their India visit accordingly.

Osstem Implant System

We use the internationally recognized Osstem Implant System for implant treatment where clinically appropriate.

Digital Dentistry

Digital X-rays, intraoral scanning and digital patient management help streamline diagnosis, treatment planning and communication.

For Patients Living Abroad

Your time matters. Your treatment deserves careful planning.

Whether you are visiting India for a few weeks or have only a limited window for dental treatment, our aim is to provide well-planned, efficient and comprehensive dental care without compromising clinical standards.
